Pharma B2B Suite

Onboard partner pharmacies, control restricted items, sell kits and keep drug data on every product, all inside Odoo.

One install. Four modules. No manual setup order to remember.

Everything installs together

Installing Pharma B2B Suite installs all four modules in the right order.

B2B Onboarding

Pharmacies apply online, sign a contract on screen, and get a portal login once you approve them.

Item Approval

Orders with controlled items or prescription products are held until they are approved or documented.

BOM & Kits

Sell compounds and kits made of components, priced from each pharmacy's own pricelist.

Product Fields

DIN, generic and brand name, manufacturer code, schedule, MLP, LCAP and tier on every product.

The customer journey

From first contact to a released order, every step is tracked.

1
Apply
The pharmacy fills in the public registration form.
2
Sign
It signs the contract on screen and a PDF is generated.
3
Activate
You review, choose a pricelist and delivery fee, and approve.
4
Order
The pharmacy orders on your website. Credit is checked.
5
Approve
Controlled items hold the order until it is approved.
6
Release
A one-time code released by you completes the order.

What you get

Onboarding

Turn applications into approved B2B customers

A public form collects the pharmacy's details and licence. After the contract is signed on screen, your team reviews the application and approves or rejects it with a reason.

✓  Registration form with licence number and licence document upload
✓  On-screen contract signature and generated PDF
✓  Approval creates the customer and a portal login
✓  Applies the pricelist (discount tier) and delivery fee you choose
✓  Welcome and rejection emails sent automatically
✓  Credit-limit check at website checkout

Approvals

Hold sensitive orders until they are cleared

Mark products as controlled or as requiring a prescription. Any order that contains them is held, and only released once the right conditions are met.

✓  Per-product flags: requires approval, requires prescription
✓  Order states: Pending Prescription and Pending Approval
✓  Pharmacies upload prescriptions from their portal
✓  Six-digit one-time code, valid for 24 hours, emailed to the pharmacy
✓  Only a hash of the code is stored, and unused codes expire automatically every hour
 
Orders with controlled or prescription items, and their prescription progress
 
Kits

Sell compounds and kits, priced per pharmacy

Define a kit from its components and choose how it behaves on the order. The kit price is built from the component prices in the customer's own pricelist.

✓  Show components on the order, or explode the kit into components on confirmation
✓  Component quantities, with an option for fixed quantities
✓  Kit price calculated from the partner's pricelist
✓  Clearer pricelist views with quantity breaks

Product data

Keep pharmacy data on the product

Adds the fields pharmacies and auditors expect to see, right on the product form.

✓  DIN (Drug Identification Number)
✓  Generic name and brand name
✓  Manufacturer code and schedule category
✓  MLP (Max Listed Price) and LCAP (Lowest Alternative Price)
✓  Tier
 
The Pharmacy Fields tab on the product form
 

Get started

Requirements

Odoo 18.0
Installs automatically: Sales, Inventory, Invoicing, Website, eCommerce, Portal, Discuss
An outgoing email server (for welcome, rejection and approval-code emails)

Install

1. Put all five module folders in your addons path
2. Update the Apps list
3. Install Pharma B2B Suite

Good to know

Uninstalling the suite does not remove the four modules
You can also install any single module on its own (the modules it needs come with it)
