Pharma Item Approval

Hold orders that contain controlled items or prescription products until they are approved or documented.

What it does

Product flags

Mark a product as requiring approval, a prescription, or both.

Held orders

Orders with flagged items move to Pending Prescription or Pending Approval instead of confirming.

Prescription upload

Pharmacies upload prescriptions from their portal, and the order continues when all are in.

 

One-time code

Approval issues a six-digit code, valid for 24 hours and emailed to the pharmacy.

Safe by design

Only a hash of the code is stored, and unused codes expire automatically every hour.

Portal pages

Pharmacies see their pending approvals and prescription uploads in their account.

See it in action

Orders with controlled or prescription items, and their prescription progress
 

How a held order is released

1
Order placed
Contains a flagged item
2
Held

3
Approved
You approve, a code is emailed
4
Released
The pharmacy enters the code
