Pharma BOM & Kit Management

Sell kits and compounds made of components, priced from each pharmacy's own pricelist.

What it does

Kit definitions

Define a kit from its components and quantities, with an option for fixed quantities.

Two behaviours

Keep the kit as one line and show its components, or explode it into components when the order is confirmed.

Price from components

The kit price is calculated from component prices using the customer's pricelist.

 

Per-pharmacy pricing

Use Odoo's native pricelists with clearer views and quantity breaks.

On the order

Each order line shows how many components a kit contains.

On the product

A BOM / Kit tab on the product form links products to their kit definition.

See it in action

Kit definitions and how each one behaves on the order
 

Requirements

✓  Odoo 18.0
✓  Pharma B2B Onboarding and Pharma Item Approval (installed automatically)
✓  Sales and Inventory (installed automatically)
