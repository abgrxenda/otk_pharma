Pharma B2B Onboarding

Let pharmacies apply online, sign your contract on screen, and become approved B2B customers with a portal login.

What it does

Public registration

A website form collects business details, licence number and licence document.

Digital contract

The applicant signs on screen and a signed PDF contract is generated and kept on the record.

Review and decide

Approve or reject each application. A rejection needs a reason, which is included in the email.

 

Automatic account setup

Approval creates the customer, a portal login, the chosen pricelist and the delivery fee.

Emails included

Welcome and rejection emails are sent for you.

Credit at checkout

Approved pharmacies can confirm on credit at website checkout, within their credit limit.

✓  Odoo 18.0
✓  Website, eCommerce, Sales, Invoicing and Portal (installed automatically)
